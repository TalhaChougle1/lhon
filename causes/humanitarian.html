OFFICIAL CAUSE MISSION

“We identify crucial needs and provide humanitarian aid where it is needed most.”

INTRODUCTION TO THE CAUSE

Humanitarian aid is the cornerstone of the Lions and Leo Club movement. Wherever social gaps leave vulnerable children, struggling elderly citizens, or underprivileged families in acute distress, volunteers mobilize to meet immediate human needs with compassion, dignity, and direct action.

At Leo Hands of Nurture, our definition of humanitarian service goes far beyond material distribution. We believe in being present — showing up consistently, building relationships of trust with local institutions, and ensuring that underserved populations in Makassar know their community cares for them.

WHY THIS CAUSE MATTERS

It is easy to donate surplus goods; it is far more meaningful and impactful to invest time, listen, and show genuine human presence. Many community institutions, particularly local orphanages and community shelters, face resource constraints and isolation from broader social networks.

By conducting regular visits, sharing meals, organizing activities, and providing mentorship, youth volunteers provide emotional encouragement that boosts childhood self-worth and creates an enduring culture of empathy across our city.

RELEVANT LHON INITIATIVES

Leo Hands of Nurture delivers regular humanitarian programs throughout Makassar:

• Orphanage Support & Community Visits: Regular weekend visits to local orphanages where volunteers lead recreational activities, share meals, and mentor young residents.

• Seasonal Donation & Giveback Drives: Collecting and distributing clothing, hygiene essentials, learning supplies, and dry rations for underprivileged families in need across the city.

GENUINE STORIES & FIELD PERSPECTIVE

FROM THE FIELD: BEING PRESENT ONGOING INITIATIVE

“Leo Hands of Nurture doesn't treat community support as a transaction. We visit. We sit down. We listen. We share meals, play games, and spend real time with the children and families we serve. The heart of our work is human connection.”
