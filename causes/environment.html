OFFICIAL CAUSE MISSION

“We find ways to protect the environment, creating healthier communities and a more sustainable world.”

INTRODUCTION TO THE CAUSE

A thriving natural environment is the foundation for human survival, public health, and sustainable development. Lions and Leos worldwide take decisive action to preserve natural habitats, reduce municipal and plastic waste, plant native trees, and protect vulnerable water systems.

In Makassar and South Sulawesi, rapid urbanization, plastic waste in coastal channels, and urban heat pose serious daily challenges for residents. Leo Hands of Nurture mobilizes local youth to take ownership of environmental health through hands-on volunteer action and sustainability education.

WHY THIS CAUSE MATTERS

Environmental degradation directly impacts the most vulnerable members of society through contaminated waterways, poor air quality, and urban flooding caused by clogged drainage infrastructure. Sustainable environmental practices cannot be achieved through policy alone — they require active community participation and conscious daily habits.

When young volunteers actively lead cleanups, waste segregation projects, and conservation campaigns, they demonstrate practical environmental responsibility to peers and establish habits that protect our shared home for decades to come.

RELEVANT LHON INITIATIVES

Leo Hands of Nurture engages youth volunteers in environmental conservation across Makassar:

• Community Cleanups & Waste Management: Mobilizing youth teams to conduct neighborhood cleanups, educate families on waste sorting, and reduce single-use plastic consumption.

• Environmental Literacy & Youth Action: Incorporating eco-friendly awareness sessions into our literacy and student workshops to inspire young environmental stewards.
