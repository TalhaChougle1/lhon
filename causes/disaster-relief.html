OFFICIAL CAUSE MISSION

“We help meet immediate needs and provide long-term support for communities affected by natural disasters.”

INTRODUCTION TO THE CAUSE

Natural disasters can upend entire communities within moments, displacing families, destroying essential infrastructure, and leaving vulnerable populations in urgent need of clean water, emergency sustenance, and medical relief. Through Lions Clubs International and the Lions Clubs International Foundation (LCIF), Lions and Leos have stood at the forefront of disaster response worldwide for decades.

In Indonesia, where communities frequently experience severe weather events, flooding, and seismic disturbances, rapid local volunteer readiness is vital. Leo Hands of Nurture engages young people in Makassar to stand ready as proactive responders and logistics volunteers when disaster impacts our region.

WHY THIS CAUSE MATTERS

Effective disaster response requires both speed in the immediate aftermath and steadfast dedication during prolonged reconstruction. While major emergency agencies provide initial rescue efforts, community volunteer networks bridge critical gaps by organizing emergency care packages, sorting vital donations, and helping displaced families reestablish everyday routines.

Youth volunteers provide essential energy, rapid organizational coordination, and digital communication capabilities that help mobilize emergency donation drives and logistics operations rapidly when every hour counts.

RELEVANT LHON INITIATIVES

Leo Hands of Nurture maintains readiness and participates in relief coordination across South Sulawesi:

• Emergency Collection & Packing Drives: Mobilizing youth volunteers to collect, categorize, and pack essential non-perishable goods, hygiene items, and blankets for affected families.

• District Network Collaboration: Coordinating with Lions District 307 B2 Indonesia and local emergency response partners to support verified logistics and distribution channels.
