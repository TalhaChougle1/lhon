OFFICIAL CAUSE MISSION

“We work to reduce the prevalence of diabetes and improve quality of life for people living with diabetes.”

INTRODUCTION TO THE CAUSE

Diabetes is one of the most pressing chronic health challenges of the 21st century, affecting hundreds of millions of people worldwide. As one of Lions International’s primary service pillars, Lions and Leos across the world take proactive steps to educate communities, promote lifestyle modifications, and expand access to preventative screenings.

In urban centers like Makassar, shifting dietary patterns and increasingly sedentary routines mean that diabetes and pre-diabetes are impacting younger demographics than ever before. Leo Hands of Nurture empowers youth to take charge of their health and advocate for preventative wellness in their families and schools.

WHY THIS CAUSE MATTERS

Type 2 diabetes is largely preventable or manageable when recognized early. However, because early-stage diabetes often develops without noticeable symptoms, millions live undiagnosed until serious complications — including vision loss, cardiovascular damage, and neuropathy — occur.

Youth-led advocacy plays a unique and potent role in breaking generational cycles of chronic illness. By sharing accessible, evidence-based nutrition knowledge and fostering active lifestyles, young volunteers inspire lasting behavioral change within their homes and broader community networks.

RELEVANT LHON INITIATIVES

Leo Hands of Nurture integrates diabetes awareness into its ongoing health portfolio across Makassar:

• Diabetes Awareness Campaigns: Digital and community educational initiatives raising awareness about balanced nutrition, early indicators of insulin resistance, and the importance of routine physical activity.

• Healthcare Advocacy in Makassar: Coordinating community health discussions alongside vision camps and public wellness days to emphasize how diabetes prevention safeguards long-term eyesight and general vitality.
