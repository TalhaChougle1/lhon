OFFICIAL CAUSE MISSION

“We support young people so they can make positive choices, lead healthy and productive lives, and become the next generation of service leaders.”

INTRODUCTION TO THE CAUSE

Young people are not merely future leaders — they are dynamic, compassionate changemakers right now. Under the Leo Club program founded by Lions International in 1957, young volunteers worldwide develop leadership skills, organize high-impact community programs, and build lifelong values of altruism and civic engagement.

At Leo Hands of Nurture in Makassar, youth empowerment is both our mission and our defining operational reality. Our executive team, departmental leaders, and project volunteers are high school students and young leaders who design, finance, and execute community service directly in our home city.

WHY THIS CAUSE MATTERS

When young people are given authentic responsibility and community support, they develop resilient problem-solving skills, empathy, communication capabilities, and civic pride. Investing in youth leadership prevents isolation, channels creative potential toward social good, and creates role models for younger children.

Through peer mentorship and educational outreach like English literacy sessions, Leo volunteers help children in under-resourced schools and orphanages see possibilities beyond their current circumstances, inspiring them to dream bigger and pursue their educational goals with confidence.

RELEVANT LHON INITIATIVES

Leo Hands of Nurture translates youth leadership into tangible programs across Makassar:

• English Literacy Program: Bringing structured English language sessions directly to children in underserved schools, orphanages, and community centers in Makassar to build confidence and open future academic horizons.

• Youth Action Programs & Volunteer Days: Mobilizing young students to take complete ownership of planning, organizing logistics, and delivering community service programs across the city.

• Leadership Development: Structured departmental operations spanning Executive, Social Media, and Human Resources teams led by youth ages 16–17 in Makassar.

FROM THE FIELD: OPENING DOORS

ENGLISH LITERACY AT LOCAL ORPHANAGES ONGOING PROGRAM

“Every Saturday morning, a group of Leo Hands of Nurture volunteers arrives at a local orphanage in Makassar with lesson materials, games, and enthusiasm. English opens doors. It builds confidence in students who want to compete in a wider world. Our volunteers don't just teach language — they show up, week after week, as friends and mentors.”
