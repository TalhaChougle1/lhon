OFFICIAL CAUSE MISSION

“We help prevent avoidable blindness and improve quality of life for people who are blind or visually impaired.”

INTRODUCTION TO THE CAUSE

Vision is the historic signature mission of Lions Clubs International, established in 1925 when Helen Keller challenged Lions to become “Knights of the Blind in the crusade against darkness.” For over a century, Lions and Leos across the globe have conducted vision screenings, equipped eye clinics, distributed prescription glasses, and worked tirelessly to eliminate preventable visual impairment.

In Makassar and South Sulawesi, uncorrected refractive errors prevent students from reading blackboards, hinder adult employment opportunities, and restrict the independence of seniors. Leo Hands of Nurture makes comprehensive vision care and free prescription glasses accessible to all members of our community.

WHY THIS CAUSE MATTERS

More than 2.2 billion people globally suffer from visual impairment or blindness, of which at least 1 billion cases could have been prevented or remain unaddressed. For a child, poor vision is often misinterpreted as a learning difficulty, causing them to fall behind in school. For adults and seniors, uncorrected sight loss creates safety hazards and limits everyday livelihoods.

A simple, high-quality pair of prescription eyeglasses is one of the most cost-effective and life-changing interventions in public health. By restoring clear sight, we restore educational opportunities, productivity, confidence, and human potential.

RELEVANT LHON INITIATIVES

Vision care is a premier operational focus of Leo Hands of Nurture in Makassar:

• Free Eye Screening Camps: Professional acuity examinations, near-vision reading tests, and refractive assessments delivered directly to community members and youth in Makassar.

• Custom Prescription Glasses Distribution: Handing over tailored prescription lenses and frames free of charge to individuals who cannot afford commercial optical care.
