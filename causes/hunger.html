OFFICIAL CAUSE MISSION

“We work to improve food security and access to nutritious food to help alleviate hunger.”

INTRODUCTION TO THE CAUSE

Access to wholesome, nourishing food is a fundamental prerequisite for human dignity, childhood learning, and active living. Around the world, Lions and Leos confront hunger by distributing emergency food packages, partnering with community food banks, and preparing nutritious meals for vulnerable populations.

In Makassar, food insecurity often affects children in under-resourced orphanages, marginalized informal settlements, and low-income households. Leo Hands of Nurture coordinates youth volunteers to collect essential groceries, deliver wholesome meals, and advocate for food security.

WHY THIS CAUSE MATTERS

Proper childhood nutrition directly shapes physical growth, brain development, and school performance. Children who experience persistent hunger face severe difficulties concentrating in class, compromising their educational prospects and reinforcing economic vulnerability.

Direct community food assistance provides immediate relief to families experiencing financial stress. When youth organize food packaging drives and share meals with children in local institutions, they ensure that community members feel seen, supported, and valued.

RELEVANT LHON INITIATIVES

Leo Hands of Nurture addresses hunger through targeted community programs in Makassar:

• Food Donation & Giveback Drives: Collecting rice, cooking oil, canned goods, and essential staples through seasonal community drives to restock kitchen pantries at local orphanages and shelters.

• Shared Meals During Community Visits: Sponsoring and serving balanced, freshly prepared meals during our regular weekend outreach visits to children’s homes across Makassar.
