OFFICIAL CAUSE MISSION

“We support the needs of children and families affected by childhood cancer.”

INTRODUCTION TO THE CAUSE

Childhood cancer is a life-altering reality that affects thousands of infants, children, and adolescents across the globe each year. As one of Lions International’s primary global causes, Leo Club members rally to bring hope, social compassion, and community solidarity to young patients and their households during their most challenging moments.

At Leo Hands of Nurture in Makassar, we believe no child or family should have to endure the emotional, physical, and psychological strain of a cancer diagnosis in isolation. Our youth volunteers are dedicated to mobilizing empathy, raising community awareness, and advocating for accessible pediatric support.

WHY THIS CAUSE MATTERS

Unlike adult cancers, pediatric cancers often develop suddenly without known lifestyle risk factors. Treatment regimens are intensive and frequently require extended hospital stays, emotional separation, and significant burdens on caregivers and families.

Community support systems play an invaluable role alongside clinical treatment. By raising awareness, fostering patient morale, and promoting community solidarity, youth-led volunteer organizations provide an indispensable layer of care that uplifts spirits and encourages families throughout their medical journeys.

RELEVANT LHON INITIATIVES

As part of our broader healthcare and community advocacy across Makassar, Leo Hands of Nurture participates in awareness campaigns and community support initiatives:

• Healthcare Awareness Campaigns: Educating youth and community members on recognizing pediatric health warning signs, advocating for proactive healthcare visits, and reducing stigma surrounding chronic childhood illnesses.

• Family and Patient Solidarity: Planning community outreach programs, emotional encouragement drives, and care initiatives alongside healthcare allies and community centers.
